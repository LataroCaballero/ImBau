<objective>
Stand up the seed's deterministic-idempotency foundation and populate the catalog + pricing of the "Brigos Recoleta" building: the `@imbau/db` seed scaffold (`seed.ts` + `runSeed()`), the `seedId`/`makePrng` id core, the D-05 fail-fast prerequisite guard, curated es-AR content constants, and the building + pricing row generators. This plan delivers SEED-01 (org + publicado project + ~13 floors + 30-40 units with a realistic pozo sale curve) and SEED-02 (two price_lists, integer-USD unit_prices, CAC payment_plans with Refuerzo[], 12-24-month cac_index history).

Purpose: Establish the single mechanism (deterministic UUIDv5 ids + `onConflictDoNothing`) that makes the whole seed re-runnable, and the owner→withTenant access split that keeps every tenant write on the production RLS path. Everything in plans 02 and 03 builds on `seed.ts`, `ids.ts`, and `content.ts` created here.
Output: A runnable (partial) `pnpm db:seed` that bootstraps the org/project and writes floors, units, price_lists, unit_prices, payment_plans, and cac_index — all idempotent, all money-correct.
</objective>
