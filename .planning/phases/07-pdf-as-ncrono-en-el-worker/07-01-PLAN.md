<objective>
Lay the worker foundation for server-side PDF rendering and build the genuinely-new piece: the
react-pdf document. Add the PDF/QR/React deps to the worker, commit a permissive embedded TTF (the
only way to get correct es-AR accents in the Chromium-free Alpine worker — PDF-02), copy the font
into the Docker runner image, declare the new `WEB_PUBLIC_BASE_URL` env var (needed by wave-2 for the
QR deep-link, D-08), and author `QuoteDoc` — a neutral, minimalist (D-06) one-page A4 document that
renders the `PdfModel` lines plus both legal leyendas (D-09/PDF-03) and a footer deep-link + QR slot.

Purpose: react-pdf is the one new technology in this phase; the #1 failure mode is the font asset not
resolving in Alpine (accents break silently). This plan de-risks that with a real render test before
any consumer wiring exists.
Output: worker deps + committed font + Dockerfile COPY + WEB_PUBLIC_BASE_URL env + `quote-pdf-doc.ts` + its render test.
</objective>
