<objective>
Stand up the worker consumer that turns a queued lead-created event into a single notification email:
read the lead + project under the payload's tenant, resolve the recipient (configured email or org-owner
fallback), send via the Plan-02 dispatch, and report failures observably.

Purpose: closes the LEADS-04 loop (the enqueue seam in Plan 03 pushes; this drains) with the same
idempotency + observability guarantees as the quote-pdf pipeline. Output: `lead-email.ts` processor +
failure reporter, worker registration in `index.ts`, worker `@imbau/api` dependency, and a unit test.
</objective>

| worker → Resend | outbound email to the resolved recipient |
